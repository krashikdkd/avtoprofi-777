Useful to know

Price questions

Are parts included in these prices?

Where a row specifies labor, parts and fluids are priced separately. We explain the complete cost for your car before the work is agreed.

Why is the final price sometimes different?

Vehicle design, wheel size, part choice and what we find during inspection can change the scope. We discuss any additional work and its price before starting it.

Can you tell what a warning light will cost to fix?

Diagnostics are the first step. A warning light points us toward a system, but the checks identify the cause and let us explain the repair and its cost.

How long should I allow for tire fitting or alignment?

Allow around an hour for a straightforward set of four tires. Wheel alignment usually takes around one to two hours.
