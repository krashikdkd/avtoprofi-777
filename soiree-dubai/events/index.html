04 / Before you join us

Make room for the night

Walk-ins are welcome when space allows. For evenings, weekends and programme nights, booking ahead is the easier way to make plans.

Should we reserve for an event?

Yes, booking is strongly advised for live music, ladies’ nights, special events and larger groups. Walk-in availability depends on the night.

What is included in a wine and cheese evening?

A curated selection of wines, artisan cheeses and pairing bites in a relaxed tasting setting. The price usually ranges from AED 150–250 per person, depending on the selection and programme.

Are ladies’ night offers always the same?

No. Ladies’ nights usually include complimentary drinks or special drink packages for women, alongside selected food offers and entertainment. Exact inclusions and prices vary by event and are announced in advance.

Can we bring a larger group?

Yes. Soirée welcomes intimate groups and larger parties, depending on the area and date. For a celebration or corporate gathering, explore private events to find out how reserved seating, drinks and sharing menus can be arranged.
